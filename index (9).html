Supreme Innovation

Technologies That Set Us Apart

Supreme Griham's exclusive innovations — unavailable with any other brand in India.

🔬
First in India
DecoShield Technology
Revolutionary surface engineering that brings uPVC to life with realistic wood grains, stone finishes, marble effects and designer patterns fused directly onto the profile.
  • Natural-looking texture with real depth & character
  • UV-resistant — no fading, peeling or discolouration
  • Scratch & weather resistant for Indian conditions
  • Uniform finish even at corners and joints
✨
New Benchmark
Gold Line Architecture
India's first metallic gold-coloured architectural groove on uPVC profiles — adding royal opulence to white, black and dark grey frames.
  • Elegant contrast — like premium jewellery for your home
  • UV-resistant, anti-tarnish — gold lasts for decades
  • Subtle 2mm or bold wide-casing options
  • Complements gold handles, locks & curtain rods
🔐
Smart Home Ready
Smart Lock & Automation
Transform your home with intelligent door & window systems integrated with app control, Alexa voice commands and biometric access management.
  • App control from anywhere in the world
  • Alexa voice command integration
  • Smart code handle & keyless entry
  • Access management for family & staff
💧
Engineering Innovation
Co-Extruded Gasket
Industry-first gasket permanently fused to the uPVC profile during extrusion — creating an airtight, watertight molecular bond that never shrinks or pulls away.
  • Zero shrinkage — sealed at molecular level
  • Ultimate monsoon-proof weatherproofing
  • Enhanced acoustic & thermal insulation
  • Maintenance-free lifetime performance
Why uPVC

Built for India.
Engineered for Life.

Every advantage of Supreme Griham uPVC is designed around real challenges faced by Gurugram & Delhi homeowners.

❄️
Energy Saving
Multi-chamber profiles and tight sealing reduce heat transfer — keeping rooms cooler in Delhi summers, cutting AC bills significantly.
🔇
40dB Noise Reduction
Precision sealing with acoustic glass blocks Gurugram traffic, construction and city noise — creating peaceful, calm interiors.
🌧️
Rainwater Insulation
Fusion-welded joints and drainage paths prevent water seepage during intense monsoons. Walls stay stain-free, interiors stay dry.
💨
Dust & Pollution Shield
Tight sealing keeps Delhi's dust, pollution and outdoor contaminants outside — maintaining cleaner, healthier indoor air quality.
🔒
Enhanced Security
Reinforced multi-chambered profiles with multi-point locking systems provide structural integrity and maximum protection for your family.
🐜
Termite Safe
uPVC doesn't attract pests, doesn't rot, and requires no chemical treatments — unlike wood, delivering durability without ongoing costs.
✨
Zero Maintenance
No painting, no polishing, no seasonal drama. A simple wipe keeps them looking new year after year — saving time and money.
🌿
Eco-Friendly
Made from fully recyclable uPVC with eco-friendly manufacturing — a sustainable choice for the planet and your home's long-term value.
